What engineers ask us first.

What does the free trial include?
Your first sheet [or: one project level / package] at no cost. Set the scope yourself. You judge it against your own standard before deciding anything.
Who stamps the drawings?
Your PE, exactly as with any outsourced production. For rebar detailing, the submittal goes to the Engineer of Record for review as usual. We don’t stamp or seal drawings.
Who does the work?
Structural engineers based in Israel, working on software we build ourselves. They are not US-licensed, which is why the stamp stays with your PE.
How fast is turnaround?
We agree a delivery date with you before we start. We’re in beta, and turnaround depends on scope.
Will you work to our standards?
Yes. Send your title blocks, layer standards, Revit templates and a typical past set, and we match them. We deliver DWG, RVT and PDF.
Are our files kept confidential?
We sign your NDA before you send anything, and files are used only for your project.
How is it priced?
After the free trial, pricing is set per firm based on your volume.
